About Physioplus

Physioplus Physiotherapy Centre treats pain, injury and loss of movement with hands-on therapy and guided exercise. Every plan starts with an assessment, so your care matches your condition and your goals.

Whether you are recovering from an operation, living with a long-term condition or want to stay active as you age, we work with you toward moving comfortably again.

Treatments and conditions we handle

Your physiotherapist will tell you which of these suits your condition after assessment.

Orthopaedic conditions

Joint, muscle, back and neck problems, with mobility and strengthening exercise.

Post surgical rehab

Step-by-step recovery after surgery: range of motion, then strength.

Ligament injury and ligament surgeries

Rehabilitation for ligament injuries and recovery after ligament surgery.

Nerve injuries

Support for movement and function after nerve injury.

Neurological conditions

Stroke, Parkinson's, GBS, myasthenia gravis and neuropathies.

Ante natal and post natal

Pregnancy exercises and recovery care after delivery.

Chest physiotherapy

For conditions such as bronchitis and for post covid rehab.

Cupping, kinesiotaping and dry needling

Specialised techniques used where your clinician finds them suitable.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition. Your physiotherapist will explain the plan after assessing you.

Is the appointment confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form sends a request. Your appointment is confirmed only when the clinic replies to you.

Do you offer home visits?

You can ask about a home visit in the appointment form. The clinic will tell you if it is possible.
